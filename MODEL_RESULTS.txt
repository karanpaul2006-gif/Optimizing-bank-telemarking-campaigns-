INTERPRETATION

1.  Both displayed confusion matrices show high specificity but low
    recall.
2.  Matrix A has 7.98% recall.
3.  Matrix B has 16.31% recall.
4.  Neither displayed matrix demonstrates the intended validation recall
    target of at least 80%.
5.  The printed 89.83% accuracy does not reconcile with Matrix B.
6.  The validation threshold objective must not be presented as achieved
    until the validation output confirms it.
7.  Validation performance and final test performance must be reported
    separately.
